Third-party programs in this package (sdk/bin)
=============================================

The P3P3DS Builder runs these programs unmodified. They are separate programs with their own
licenses; the Builder's own code is under the MIT License (LICENSE.txt).


FFmpeg (ffmpeg, ffprobe)
------------------------
Converts the game's music and movies on your computer.
This is a GPL build of FFmpeg: it includes the x264 H.264 encoder (GNU GPL version 2 or later),
so the build as a whole is licensed under the GNU General Public License. The license files of
the build, when it ships them, are in this package as FFMPEG_*.
Source code:
  FFmpeg  https://ffmpeg.org/download.html  (https://git.ffmpeg.org/ffmpeg.git)
  x264    https://code.videolan.org/videolan/x264
The build's own download page lists the exact versions and build scripts.
